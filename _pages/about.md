[Lifu Tu](https://lifu-tu.github.io/) is a research scientist at Salesforce AI Research. He earned his Ph.D. from [TTI-Chicago](https://ttic.edu/), a philanthropically endowed academic computer science institute located on the [University of Chicago](https://www.uchicago.edu/) campus. During PhD, I was advised by [Kevin Gimpel](https://home.ttic.edu/~kgimpel/index.html).

His current research focuses on grounding for LLMs: multilingual and multimodal generation, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and methods to enhance factual consistency in large language model outputs. Beyond language, he explores cross-modal innovation—most notably text-to‑video synthesis (as demonstrated in the xGen‑VideoSyn‑1 model) — and structured prediction techniques for richer, more reliable generation systems.

The following are some of my previous projects:
- [multilingual retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14274)
- [LLM benchmarking](https://www.salesforceairesearch.com/crm-benchmark)
- [Long video generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12590)
- [Code generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.13474)
